Our laboratory and the WIDE Project are joining [Measurement Lab](http://www.measurementlab.net/), led by the New America Foundation, PlanetLab, Google and others. By contributing measurement techniques for Internet quality worldwide, we aim to understand the current state of network transparency and to think about what healthy, sustainable development of the Internet means.
