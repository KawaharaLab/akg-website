The large amount of running cost at vertical farming makes payment difficult. Although some companies continuously make a profit by high productivity resulted from sophisticated control strategy of the environment or “recipe”, it still requires many trials and errors in research and development. In this study, we propose a novel way to improve recipe based on time series data analysis, which no longer requires trials and errors, using Personal Food Computer developed by OpenAg.
