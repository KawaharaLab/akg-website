Smartphones equipped with various sensors are useful for sensing activities. However, because smartphones greedily consume power, the battery lifetime is a serious problem. In this research, we exploit a feature of Android devices which allows them to boot up automatically when connected to a charger. We propose a system to realize the intermittent operation by powering the target smartphone with microwave power transfer. We aim at developing a technology to selectively trigger the boot process in multiple devices.
