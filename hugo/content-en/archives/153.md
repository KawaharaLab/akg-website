Ryo Shigeta (M2) was recognized with the Student Paper Competition Award, Second Place, at IEEE Sensors 2012 for the paper "Ambient-RF-Energy-Harvesting Sensor Node with Capacitor-Leakage-Aware Duty Cycle Control", co-authored with the Georgia Institute of Technology.
