Model case

We aim to build safety confirmation system by SNS in a situation that communications among shelters are difficult such as in a disaster because of line disconnections and other reasons. A model was proposed for this case \[2\], based on COPSS\[1\], a publish/subscribe system embedded in Information Centric Network(ICN). They let a car, equipped with a special router and a cache (called DataMule), circulate among shelters and relay messages. This system has drawbacks of a long delivery time and a single-point-of-failure at Randezvous Point(RP). To solve these problems, we study a new method to improve the performance and achieve the robustness against the failure of RP\[3\], introducing the shortcut delivery of publish message to subscribers via the middle nodes. It realizes a shorter delivery time, resistance to RP failure and also saving energy.  
In the future, we plan to discuss the safety against tampering of delivery and the usefulness of this method.

\[1\]J. Chen et al., “COPSS: An Efficient Content Oriented Publish/Subscribe System,” The 2011 Seventh ACM/IEEE Symposium on Architectures for Networking and Communications Systems  
, pp.99-110, 2011.
