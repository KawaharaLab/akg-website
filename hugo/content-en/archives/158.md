Polarization controllable retrodirective antenna

The weight of a spacecraft is critical to the launcing cost. Eliminating all the cables including power cables is reported to be able to reduce 10% of the spacecraft’s weight. To replace all the cables connected to sensors, JAXA proposed a wireless sensor network combined with wireless power transfer (WPT) and energy harvesting by microwave.  
In a spacecraft, it is difficult to uniformly transfer wireless power to all the sensors, because of many devices installed. Controlling the beam from the antenna achieves effective wireless power transfer. We fabricated a retrodirective antenna using mixer for phase conjugation to transmit a signal back toward the direction of the incoming signal. Retrodirective antenna has a drawback of a interference loss between incoming and outgoing signals. Minimizing this requires to make polarization orthogonal.  
We aim the performance improvement of Hybrid Integrated Circuit (HIC) for retrodirective antenna. Specifically we enhance the isolation of diplexer, make the mixer using FET have higher conversion gain, fabricate Active Integrated Antenna (AIA), and develop the control system for WPT power management. Furthermore, based on this result, we also consider the fabrication of MMIC using RF-CMOS technology.
