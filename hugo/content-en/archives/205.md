Associate Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara, together with Microsoft Research (UK) and the Georgia Institute of Technology (USA), developed a technique for printing a variety of electronic circuit elements with a home inkjet printer. By making clever use of off-the-shelf equipment and materials, circuit boards, touch sensors and antennas can now be made instantly with inexpensive equipment. The paper received the Best Paper Award at ACM UbiComp 2013 for showing that, combined with the spread of 3D printers and open-source hardware, ordinary people will be able to make sophisticated electronic circuits with ease.
