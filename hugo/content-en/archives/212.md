[AgIC](http://agic.cc) has been founded to commercialize [Instant Inkjet Circuits](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=2493486), a circuit-printing technique that uses inkjet printing of silver nanoparticle ink. Associate Professor Kawahara also serves as a technical advisor to AgIC.
