Although Instant Inkjet Circuits have enabled us to print electric circuit on a paper substrate with an off-the-shelf inkjet printer, this technique is limited to special coated paper like the one from Mitsubishi Paper Mills. This leads to difficulty in meeting diverse demands for substrates such as heat tolerance, biodegradability, and so on. We achieved a novel way to transcribe silver nano-particle ink from a paper substrate to many kinds of sticky materials, by deliberately choosing a “less stable” paper for silver to stick to.
