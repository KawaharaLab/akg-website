Traditionally, fabrication of electronic circuits involves either expensive, troublesome, and time-consuming processes. In our research, we propose using silver nano-particle ink and paper from Mitsubishi Paper Mills Ltd. with home commodity inkjet printer to rapidly prototype electronic circuits. With this fabrication method, it is possible to make complicated electronic circuits in much less time and lower price. Furthermore, bendable substrates used to print conductive patterns allow fabricating a wide range of flexible electronic circuits and applications.

Ta Duc Tung, Koya Narumi
