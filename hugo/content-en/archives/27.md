■ Purpose  
You got into IT, entered the Faculty of Engineering, and now you are in your fourth year. The time to choose a career path is coming, and yet all you hear about the IT industry is negative, like the "[ten years of mud](http://www.atmarkit.co.jp/news/200805/28/ipa.html)" remark.  
Many engineering and information-science students born around 1986 probably share that vague anxiety.  
Is the IT industry really such a thankless place, with no dreams, no money and no free time? Engineers born around 1976, ten years ahead of you, will tell it like it is, at eye level. Our guest speakers are engineers and executives working on the front lines of world-famous electronics makers, IT companies and startups.  
"You, ten years from now" will answer any question.
