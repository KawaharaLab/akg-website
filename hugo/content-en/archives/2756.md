We present a system that allows users to freely (re)construct a 2-D wireless charging surface. By simply placing our ready-made modules, we can add wireless charging capability to the surface such as desks, floor, and wall. This enables electronic devices to be charged anywhere on the surface.

Kazunobu Sumiya, Takuya Sasatani, Yuki Nishizawa, Kenji Tsushio, Yoshiaki Narusue, Yoshihiro Kawahara

The cost of deploying wireless power transfer (WPT) technology into products remains high, due to the made-to-order design process. To alleviate the engineering cost to adapt to each use case, a reconfigurable 2-D WPT system based on magnetic resonance coupling was recently proposed. This system dynamically obtains the topology of the modules via inter-module communication. However, stable communication gets difficult as the number of modules increases or other traffics co-exist. To overcome these challenges and construct a robust and scalable system, we proposed a topology construction protocol based on “in-band” communication.

Masahiro Morita, Takuya Sasatani, Ryo Takahashi, Yoshihiro Kawahara
