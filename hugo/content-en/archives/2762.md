By pasting conductive tapes to the surface of an object, we can easily build an electric pattern on it. When conductive tapes are applied three-dimensionally, the connection pattern between them changes depending on the shape of the object. By using this discontinuous change of connection and disconnection, we can tell the current shape of the object with relatively high resolution, and feedback it to control actuators without bulky microcontrollers or sensors.

Fuminori Okuya, Yoshihiro Kawahara
