Polyamine, one of the metabolites of intestinal bacteria, is reported to be a factor indicating the health condition. Therefore, monitoring and maintaining the polyamine concentration on a daily basis is expected to benefit to human health. We are developing a disposable sensor that can measure polyamine concentration in feces easily at home.

Etsushi Nozaki, Yuki Kitazumi, Jie Qi, Hirokazu Ota, Shin Kurihara, Kenji Kano, Yoshihiro Kawahara
