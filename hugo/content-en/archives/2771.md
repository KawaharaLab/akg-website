Current 3D printers are time- and support-material- consuming. If an object is 3D printed in a vertically folded state and expanded after printed, we can significantly reduce the printing time and the support material consumption. We printed a concentric popup structure in the folded state, and evaluated the relationship between the object’s bistability and its height, thickness, and curvature.

Yuta Noma, Koya Narumi, Fuminori Okuya, Yoshihiro Kawahara
