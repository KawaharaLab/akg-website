In user operation on the projector image, it is desirable to perform a pointing operation directly on the screen without holding anything in the users’ hand. We proposed Touchable Wall, a new touch-operated large-screen projection system using acoustic vibration sensing. We apply a continuous signal in the inaudible range of an actuator to a surface and acquire its elastic compliance change as a resonance of sounds. We perform “true” touch detection, position estimation, and pressure estimation by using machine learning techniques.

Takefumi Hiraki, Masaaki Fukumoto, Yoshihiro Kawahara
