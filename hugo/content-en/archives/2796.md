We proposed a soft inflatable structure driven by low air pressure and an internally mounted tendon. The outer frame is made of soft and lightweight fabric material, and the control tendon is placed inside, so that people can touch it more naturally. In this study, we constructed a theoretical model of the inflatable joint working as a basic component and verified it based on the measurement results. Using these, it is possible to design the structure and interaction of soft robots.

Young ah Seong, Ryuma Niiyama, Yoshihiro Kawahara and Yasuo Kuniyoshi
