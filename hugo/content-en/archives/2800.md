Wireless power transfer (WPT) via quasistatic cavity resonance (QSCR) proposed a route towards ubiquitous WPT, which safely supplies several Watts of power to electronic devices as they enter a WPT enabled space. However, there are still some drawbacks to this approach such as the need for a conductive pole in the middle of the room and the decay in power efficiency with increasing distance from the center of the room. To address these challenges, we propose multimode QSCR, which enables pole-less systems and efficient (\>50%) power delivery over the entire room space.

Takuya Sasatani, Alanson P. Sample, Yoshihiro Kawahara
