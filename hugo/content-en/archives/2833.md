A paper by Visiting Researcher Takefumi Hiraki and colleagues received the FY2018 Measurement Division Paper Award of the Society of Instrument and Control Engineers (SICE). The award ceremony will be held on August 30, 2019 at the Sensing Forum, the Measurement Division's annual conference.
