A paper by Project Assistant Professor Ryo Shigeta, Kouta Suzuki (then a master's student), Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara and colleagues was selected as a "Specially Selected Paper" of the IPSJ Journal. (October 15, 2019) The designation is given to papers reporting exceptionally outstanding research that more researchers should consult. <https://www.ipsj.or.jp/award/ssp_award.html>
