On October 21, 2019, as the Museum of Modern Art in New York (MoMA) reopened after its renovation, a work by Project Assistant Professor Jie Qi was shown as part of the opening exhibition and was selected for the museum's permanent collection. MoMA holds many works by masters such as Picasso, Van Gogh, Yayoi Kusama and Takashi Murakami, and is the largest and most influential museum in the world of modern art.
