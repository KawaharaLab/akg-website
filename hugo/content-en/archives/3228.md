Research by Kazunobu Sumiya (M2, graduated FY2019) and colleagues received the Distinguished Paper Award from ACM Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies (IMWUT). The award recognizes particularly outstanding papers among those accepted in 2019 by the Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies (IMWUT), the top journal in ubiquitous computing. This year six of 166 papers received it. [Awarded September 17, 2020]
