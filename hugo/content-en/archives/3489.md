Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara received the 19th DOCOMO Mobile Science Award (Advanced Technology Division, Award of Excellence). (Awarded October 2019)

His research on advancing real-world sensing technology through cross-disciplinary methods was highly evaluated, with great expectations for its future contribution to society.
