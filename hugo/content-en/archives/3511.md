Koya Narumi, formerly a third-year doctoral student in the Kawahara Laboratory, and members of the ERATO Kawahara Universal Information Network Project received the Best Paper Award Runner Up at the IEEE International Conference on Soft Robotics (RoboSoft). (Awarded June 30, 2020)

The award goes to the second-best paper at IEEE RoboSoft, the international conference on soft robotics, which accepted 130 papers this year.
