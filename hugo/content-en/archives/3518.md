Research by Hiroki Sato and Lecturer Ryuma Niiyama of the ERATO Kawahara Universal Information Network Project and colleagues received the Best Demo Award at the 33rd Annual ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST '20). (Awarded October 23, 2020)

UIST, one of the top conferences on user interfaces, was held entirely online. The Best Demo Award goes to the most outstanding presentation in the conference's demonstration track. The presentation of the accepted paper "poimo: Portable and Inflatable Mobility Devices Customizable for Personal Physical Characteristics" was highly evaluated.
