Ken Takaki and Etsushi Nozaki won the Business Plan Competition at the LES Asia Pacific Conference 2020. (Awarded October 22, 2020)

Among universities from across the Asia-Pacific region, they presented the business plan for "asEars" online. The clarity of their commercialization plan and their awareness of intellectual property, including a survey of related patents, were highly evaluated.
