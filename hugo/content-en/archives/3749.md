From May to June 2021, mercari R4D and the Research Institute for an Inclusive Society through Engineering (RIISE) will hold a symposium on "Value Exchange Engineering", the subject of their joint research. mercari R4D and RIISE conduct research every day toward a future in which everyone can exchange value fairly and safely. The symposium will present the research being done in the new field of value exchange engineering and its outlook, through conversations among the participating faculty and presentations of the research assistants' work. The symposium is held online, through videos released on YouTube and an online community on the chat tool Slack, so you can watch whenever it suits you. We look forward to your participation.
