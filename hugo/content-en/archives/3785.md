The ERATO Kawahara Universal Information Network Project pursued research and development across a wide range of fields, including IoT, energy, actuation and fabrication.

Over five and a half years the project took on many different themes, and whenever it started a new one it kept in mind how the technology would blend into our lives.

This book, by project member Hisato Ogata, asks what technology is to human beings and, taking "conviviality" as its starting point, explores what technology should be in our time.

Conversations among the leaders of the project's research areas and their results are collected in Chapter 7.

Release date: May 21, 2021
