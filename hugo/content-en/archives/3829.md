Despite recent advancements in both academic and industrial fields, research on origami structures are mainly limited to rigid panels and paper. While textiles play an essential role in human society, they were inappropriate for fabricating origami structures because of the low rigidity of the textiles and the difficulty to keep the folding patterns. In this research, we realized textile-based origami structures by utilizing 3D-printed frameworks made by PLA resin. These structures enable low-DOF (Degree of Freedom) folding patterns and controllable rigidity of textile surfaces while preserving their flexibility.

Tetsuya Toji, Yuta Noma, Koya Narumi, Kazuya Saito, Yoshihiro Kawahara
