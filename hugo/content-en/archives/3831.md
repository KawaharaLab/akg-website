ExpandFab is a fabrication method for creating expanding objects using foam materials. We investigated a basic principle of the expansion rate and developed materials by mixing foam powder and elastic adhesive along with a fabrication method using the foam materials. The 3D printer prints the expandable object by curing the printed material with ultraviolet light. Finally, the user heats the printed objects to expand them by approximately 2.7 times at maximum.

Hiroki Kaimoto, Junichi Yamaoka, Satoshi Nakamaru, Yoshihiro Kawahara, Yasuaki Kakehi
