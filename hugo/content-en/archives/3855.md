Partitions have been recently placed in evacuation centers to protect privacy, while they suffer from poor ventilation which is not desirable for preventing infectious diseases. To address this challenge, we propose the bent honeycomb structure, which enables ventilation through many hexagonal holes while blocking the field of vision towards the holes. Using this structure, we can fabricate partitions that achieve both ventilation and privacy protection, which has been difficult for existing partitions.

Akari Shimono, Yuta Noma, Yoshihiro Kawahara
