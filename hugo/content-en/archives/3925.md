We propose Micro Elastic Pouch Motors, a largely deformable and miniaturized soft actuator that is made by an elastic rubber pouch with a low-boiling-point liquid. When the temperature of the low-boiling-point liquid reaches 34 ˚C, the liquid inside the pouch evaporates, and the whole structure inflates. Thanks to the proposed fabrication method, we can make a miniaturized pouch of approximately 5 mm in diameter with a thin rubber membrane, and the pouch can inflate to a volume of 86 times or more compared to its initial volume and can generate approximately 20 N at maximum.

Seiya Hirai, Tatsuho Nagatomo, Takefumi Hiraki, Hiroki Ishizuka, Yoshihiro Kawahara, and Norihisa Miki
