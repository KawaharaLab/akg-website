We propose HaPouch, a wearable haptic display that uses a liquid-to-gas phase change actuator and a Peltier device as a way to reduce the size of the entire system. A low-boiling-point liquid is encapsulated in the flexible pouch of the actuator, and the vaporization of the liquid, which induces the inflation of the actuator, is controlled by the external Peltier device. We implemented a temperature and pressure sensor to monitor pressure inside the pouch and this measurement will contribute to controlling the generated force.

Ryusei Uramune, Hiroki Ishizuka, Takefumi Hiraki, Yoshihiro Kawahara, Sei Ikeda, and Osamu Oshiro
