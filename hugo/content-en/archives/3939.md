We will hold a final report meeting summarizing six years of research.

On September 17 there will be a talk session with the group leaders (streamed live on YouTube Live).

From October, a static exhibition of the results (including prototypes developed in the project) is planned at the University of Tokyo Mejirodai campus, by advance reservation for a specified date and time only.

We look forward to the participation of researchers, students and members of the public interested not only in information and communication but also in robotics, IoT, physics, mathematics and design.

Please join us.

■ **Friday, October 1 – Thursday, October 14, 2021: public exhibition**
