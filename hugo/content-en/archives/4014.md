The research "poimo: a soft, people-friendly electric mobility device that inflates with air" by the Niiyama Laboratory (Graduate School of Information Science and Technology), the Kawahara Laboratory (Graduate School of Engineering) and mercari R4D was selected as an outstanding technology for the "Innovative Technologies 2021 Special Prize". (Awarded November 17, 2021)

It also received two further awards, the "SIGGRAPH Award" and the "DCAJ Chairman's Award", after comprehensive evaluation on the three criteria of progressiveness, originality and market creation.
