The concept of the Ubiquitous Sensing Platform

[![picture2](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/picture2-1024x652.png)](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/picture2.png)

This platform supports those who tackle with social issues by providing the sensor data via API.

[![picture3](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/picture3-1024x657.png)](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/picture3.png)

The empty space of LTE packet (padding) enables us to upload the sensor data with no increase of traffic and power consumption.

Recent years, Cloud Services have been evolving rapidly and now they are essential to our daily life. However, the current cloud systems lack very important element, that is, the ability to know what is happening in the real world. So, we are trying to make “nerves” for them utilizing the existing smartphones and mobile networks. Concretely speaking, we measure a wide variety of environmental information with the sensor devices on smartphones, collect the sensor data via mobile network and provide the data with cloud services via APIs. But, this scheme, “Participatory Sensing”, has difficulty in recruiting enough number of participants due to the increase of traffic and power consumption. So, we proposed to utilize the empty space of LTE packet (padding) for uploading the sensor data with no increase of traffic and power consumption. We also analyzed the traffic stats from 142 users, 94.5GB, and estimated that more than 1% of traffic can be used for sensor data uploading.
