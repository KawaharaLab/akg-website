Takuya Sasatani was selected for "Innovators Under 35 Japan 2021", an award by MIT Technology Review (Japanese edition) that aims to discover and support young innovators tackling global challenges. (Awarded December 16, 2021)

The award goes to 15 innovators under 35, including entrepreneurs, researchers and activists, across seven fields: computers/electronics, software, transportation (including space development), the Internet, AI/robotics, communications, and energy/sustainability. Takuya Sasatani was recognized in the field of communications. Recipients are nominated for the following year's global "Innovators Under 35".
