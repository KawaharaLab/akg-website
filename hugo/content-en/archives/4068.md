Kazuki Koyama, a fourth-year undergraduate, received the Convention Encouragement Award (formerly the Academic Encouragement Award) and the Convention Student Encouragement Award (convention local award) at the 84th IPSJ National Convention, "A changing society and information processing". (Awarded March 3, 2022)
