On September 17 and October 1–14, 2021, we held a final report meeting summarizing six years of research.

On September 17 there was a talk session with the group leaders (streamed live on YouTube Live).

From October 1, a static exhibition of the results (including prototypes developed in the project) was held at the University of Tokyo Mejirodai campus and welcomed many visitors.
