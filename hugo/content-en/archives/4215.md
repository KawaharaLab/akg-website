Ryo Takahashi, a doctoral student, received the Best Paper Award at CHI 2022, one of the top international conferences in human-computer interaction (HCI). (The conference was held in a hybrid format in New Orleans, USA and online from April 30 to May 5, 2022; the award was announced on May 2.)
