Ryo Takahashi, a doctoral student, was named Runner Up for the Gaetano Borriello Outstanding Student Award, given to students who have made outstanding contributions to the field of ubiquitous computing, at the 2022 ACM International Joint Conference on Pervasive and Ubiquitous Computing (UbiComp 2022).

Awarded September 15, 2022
