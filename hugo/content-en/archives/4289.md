Etsushi Nozaki (master's graduate, FY2021) received the Student Presentation Award at the Spring Meeting of the Acoustical Society of Japan. Established in 2009, the award encourages young researchers who will carry the future of acoustics, and goes to student members who gave outstanding presentations at the spring or autumn meeting.

Awarded September 15, 2022
