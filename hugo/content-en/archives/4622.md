A paper by a research team from our laboratory, Ken Takaki (D3, Department of Electrical Engineering and Information Systems), Etsushi Nozaki (graduate of the Graduate School of Information Science and Technology), Tomomi Kanai (Department of Mechano-Informatics), Project Associate Professor Ari Hautasaari (Graduate School of Interdisciplinary Information Studies), Daisuke Sato (Carnegie Mellon University), Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara (Department of Electrical Engineering and Information Systems), Associate Professor Akinori Kashio (The University of Tokyo Hospital) and Professor Emeritus Tatsuya Yamasoba (The University of Tokyo Hospital), received the Honorable Mention Award at CHI 2023, the premier international conference in human-computer interaction. CHI 2023 (The ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems) is the premier international conference in human-computer interaction and was held in a hybrid format in Hamburg, Germany and online from April 23 to 28, 2023. The Honorable Mention Award goes to the top 5% of submitted papers.

Awarded April 2023
