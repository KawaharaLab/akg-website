The rise of IoT is increasing the need for wireless power transfer and low-power communication technologies. Previous studies adopt load-modulation communication for adding low-power communication capabilities to wireless power transfer systems, but it negatively affects power transfer as it needs to either allocate a time frame for data transmission or affect the load impedance. This paper shows that by modulating the receiver-side DC/DC converters, we can achieve low-power uplink communication in wireless power transfer systems without compromising power transfer performance. Evaluations show that the proposed technique enables 25 kb/s communication alongside 9.13 W power transfer and allows load impedance tuning, which is often used for maximum efficiency point tracking.

Qunchao Zhou, Takuya Sasatani, Changyo Han, Yoshihiro Kawahara
