Advances in flexible electronics enable on-body comfortable sensors that can be always worn on the body surface. However, there are still many challenges in communicating with these devices. For example, a standard clothing-type wireless reader coil generates magnetic field exposure to the body and cannot create an effective large-scale communication link on the body. Therefore, we proposed a clothing-type body-scale reader coil by employing a zigzag wiring pattern called a meander coil, which generates a strong magnetic field only near the clothing, and a highly sensitive readout circuit.

Ryo Takahashi, Wakako Yukita, Takuya Sasatani, Tomoyuki Yokota, Takao Someya, Yoshihiro Kawahara
