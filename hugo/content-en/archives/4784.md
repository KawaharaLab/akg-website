3D printing sometimes requires a connection mechanism of multiple parts for some mechanical function or assembly of a large object. Many existing connectors have problems in durability, time, and/or effort when we try to iteratively attach and detach components. We propose a free-form 3D printable zipper, which can iteratively open and close with easy manipulation. Especially, we designed and verified a zipper that moves along a curve in a two-dimensional plane.

Ryo Masuda, Koya Narumi, Yuta Noma, Kazuki Koyama, Yoshihiro Kawahara
