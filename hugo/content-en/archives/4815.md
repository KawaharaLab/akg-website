Inkjet 4D Print is a self-folding fabrication method of 3D origami tessellations by printing 2D patterns on both sides of a heat-shrinkable base sheet, using a commercialized inkjet ultraviolet (UV) printer. It has merits in (1) folding complex patterns with over 80,000 facets, (2) printing the same volume 20 times faster than an FDM 3D printer, and (3) optionally printing full-color decoration. We also developed the software that generates printing patterns folding into an input 3D model. This technique demonstrated that diverse 3D shapes are fabricable with the 2D fabrication method.

Koya Narumi\*, Kazuki Koyama\*, Koya Narumi, Kai Suto, Yuta Noma, Hiroki Sato, Tomohiro Tachi, Masaaki Sugimoto, Takeo Igarashi, Yoshihiro Kawahara (\* joint first authors)
