Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara and colleagues presented a method for prototyping circuits with a home inkjet printer loaded with silver nanoparticle ink at UbiComp 2013, where it received the Best Paper Award. Ten years on, the work was recognized as still influential and received the 10-year Impact Award.

Awarded October 1, 2023

[Instant inkjet circuits: lab-based inkjet printing to support rapid prototyping of UbiComp devices](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2493432.2493486 "https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2493432.2493486")
