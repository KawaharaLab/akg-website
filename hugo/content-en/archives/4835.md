Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara received the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry Award at the "Award for Academic Startups", which honors outstanding university-launched startups with promising futures, along with the universities and companies that contributed most to their growth.

Awarded August 24, 2023

[Award for Academic Startups secretariat, Japan Science and Technology Agency](https://www.jst.go.jp/aas/award.html "https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2493432.2493486")
