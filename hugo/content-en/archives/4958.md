Professor Yoshihiro Kawahara received the FY2024 Commendation for Science and Technology by the Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology, Prize for Science and Technology (Research Category). The prize honors individuals and groups whose original research and development is likely to contribute to the advancement of science and technology in Japan. Professor Kawahara was recognized for his "Research on advancing IoT devices".

Awarded April 17, 2024
