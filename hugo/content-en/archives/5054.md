Jamming grippers can grasp objects of various shapes with simple control. However, grasping fragile objects is challenging, as a secure grasp requires firm pressing, which can potentially damage delicate objects. Thus, it is important to achieve an appropriate pressing distance for damage-free grasping. Consequently, the detection of the deformation of objects during grasping is required. In this study, we introduced a tactile sensing method using a Permanent Magnet Elastomer (PME) membrane to detect the initial deformation of grasping objects. The initial deformation can be detected from the inflection point in magnetic field data, and delicate objects, such as roll cakes or potato chips, can be grasped without being damaged. This study enables jamming grippers to delicately grasp fragile objects without damaging them, thus extending their use in sectors, such as the food industry.

Ho Enomoto, Matthew Ishige, Takuya Umedachi, Mitsuhiro Kamezaki, Yoshihiro Kawahara
