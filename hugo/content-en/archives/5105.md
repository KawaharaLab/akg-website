The poster presentation by Project Assistant Professor Ryo Takahashi received the Best Poster Presentation Award at the 7th meeting of the Young Researchers' Society for Flexible and Stretchable Electronics. The award, decided by a vote of the program committee, goes to the top 10% of poster presentations. (Awarded May 24, 2024)
