A presentation by Ho Enomoto (M2, graduated FY2023) and colleagues received the Excellent Research and Technology Award at the 41st Annual Conference of the Robotics Society of Japan. The award selects particularly outstanding research and technology among the presentations at the conference and is given to the authors; the number of awards is capped at one per 100 presentations, plus one for each further 100. (Awarded September 5, 2024)
