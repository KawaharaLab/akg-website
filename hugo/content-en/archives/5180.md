At the International Conference on Indoor Positioning and Indoor Navigation (IPIN) 2024, held October 14–17, 2024, a paper by Yukiya Mita (M1), Hiroaki Murakami (Project Assistant Professor), Takuya Sasatani (Assistant Professor) and Yoshihiro Kawahara (Professor) of our laboratory received the Best Paper Award 1st Place. The award was selected from the 78 accepted papers.

Awarded October 17, 2024
