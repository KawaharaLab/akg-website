Yuichi Honjo (M2) received the Best Poster Presentation Award at the IEICE Cross-disciplinary Workshop on Super-intelligent Networking (RISING 2024), held in Sapporo, Hokkaido, November 11–12, 2024. About 20% of submitted presentations receive the award, and Yuichi Honjo was one of 14 recipients. (Awarded November 12, 2024)
