Project Assistant Professor Ryo Takahashi received the Micro-Nano Science and Technology Division's Excellent Presentation Paper Award at the 15th Micro-Nano Science and Technology Symposium of the Japan Society of Mechanical Engineers, held in Sendai, Miyagi, November 25–28, 2024. The award goes to roughly the top 1% of submitted presentations. (Awarded November 28, 2024)
