Project Assistant Professor Ken Takaki received the Telecom Interdisciplinary Research Student Award at the 40th Telecommunications Advancement Foundation Awards, for the paper "asEars: Designing and Evaluating the User Experience of Wearable Assistive Devices for Single-Sided Deafness". (Awarded March 26, 2025)
