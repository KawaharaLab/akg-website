![Mohammad Azka Hafizh Adziman, Nicholas Ryan Megahan and Takuro Suzuki, three international students who joined the Kawahara Laboratory as new members](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DSC05475.jpg)

Since late June, three international students, Mohammad Azka Hafizh Adziman, Nicholas Ryan Megahan and Takuro Suzuki, have been taking part in the Kawahara Laboratory as new members through the Engineering Summer Education Program of the Graduate School of Engineering and Faculty of Engineering (ESEP-G 2025 (Note 1) and ESEP-UC 2025 (Note 2)).

Adziman studies at the University of Oxford in the UK; Megahan and Suzuki study at the University of California, Irvine in the US. Their fields include engineering science and computer science.

In our laboratory they are working on Embodied AI research alongside the lab's members and have been engaging in research with great enthusiasm.

![Takuro Suzuki and Mohammad Azka Hafizh Adziman chatting after a meeting](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DSC05445.jpg)

Takuro Suzuki (left) and Mohammad Azka Hafizh Adziman (right) chatting after a meeting

![Nicholas Ryan Megahan showing his research in progress](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DSC05451.jpg)

Nicholas Ryan Megahan showing his research in progress

We hope that everyone's perspectives and expertise will inspire one another and lead to new discoveries and progress!  
We look forward to a fruitful and rewarding time together.

(Text and photographs: Reika Yoneyama)

Note 1: ESEP-G  
The Engineering Summer Education Program of the Graduate School of Engineering and Faculty of Engineering for third-year undergraduates and above from partner universities and other overseas universities and graduate schools designated by the school. Period: Tuesday, June 24 – Friday, August 1, 2025.

Note 2: ESEP-UC  
The Engineering Summer Education Program for third- and fourth-year undergraduates from the campuses of the University of California. Period: Thursday, June 19 – Wednesday, July 30, 2025.
