Since mid-June, Marwa AlAlawi has been working in the Kawahara Laboratory as a graduate international internship trainee supported by the JST ASPIRE program!

Marwa belongs to MIT CSAIL (Note 1) and specializes in HCI (human-computer interaction). She is particularly interested in how information technology and the real world connect to create new value (Note 2).

Of her time in our lab, she told us: "I look forward to expanding the possibilities further through collaboration with researchers in the same field!" She also said that convenience stores are a favorite of hers in Japan, and their convenience and quality have become one of the pleasures of her stay.

![Marwa at work](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DSC05620.jpg)

Marwa at work

From July 2 to 6 she took part as a leader in the MIT Japan Program's STEAM workshop in Kesennuma, one of many activities since arriving in Japan. We look forward to her research here!

![Marwa AlAlawi](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/DSC05616.jpg)

Note 1: MIT CSAIL  
The Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.  
Note 2: How information technology and the real world connect to create new value.  
Also called the intersection of the digital and the physical.
