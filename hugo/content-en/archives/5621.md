![全身無線給電・通信服](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/1_takahashi.jpg)

Turning clothing into an infrastructure for communication and power supply to enable the continuous, battery-free operation of numerous wearable devices is vital for the future of healthcare and human-machine interaction. However, conventional wireless power transfer methods using textile coils face a challenge: large coils cause magnetic fields to penetrate deep into human tissues, making it difficult to balance safety with transmission efficiency. This study aims to realize a network capable of efficiently supplying power and data to devices distributed across the whole body while minimizing electromagnetic exposure to the human body. To address this issue, we developed a system utilizing meander coils with a zig-zag structure. This design locally confines strong magnetic fields to the skin surface, thereby preventing exposure to deep tissues. Furthermore, because the system is physically isolated from the ambient wireless environment, it achieves high noise immunity, enabling safe and efficient wireless communication. These results provide a foundation for the semi-permanent daily use of next-generation devices such as electronic skins, serving as a key technology to support advanced physiological sensing and augmented reality (AR) experiences.  

Ryo Takahashi, Takashi Sato, Wakako Yukita, Changyo Han, John S. Ho, Eiji Iwase, Junya Kurumida, Akihito Noda, Tomoyuki Yokota, Takao Someya, Yoshihiro Kawahara
