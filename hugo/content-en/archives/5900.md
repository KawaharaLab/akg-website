On September 12, 2025 we issued a press release, "AI makes the environmental impact of each second-hand garment visible: automatically extracting the product-specific data behind environmental impact calculations from flea-market app listings".  
(Press release)  
[https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/press/pr2025-09-12-001](https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/press/pr2025-09-12-001)

**Summary**  
This research established a method for automatically estimating with AI the environmental impact (greenhouse gas emissions) of each second-hand garment listed on a flea-market app.  
From the photographs and descriptions posted by sellers, the AI reads information such as materials and number of uses with high accuracy and automatically computes a life cycle assessment (LCA). This is a major step over the "data collection barrier" that has made environmental impact assessment of products difficult, and is expected to contribute to a circular economy.  
The results were presented at the 12th International Conference on Life Cycle Management (LCM2025), held in Italy from September 9 to 12, 2025.
