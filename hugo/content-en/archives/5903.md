On October 1, 2025 we issued a press release, "A ring-shaped wireless mouse that runs on a tiny amount of power: toward an unobtrusive, semi-permanent controller for AR glasses in everyday spaces".  
In this research we developed "picoRing mouse", an ultra-low-power ring-shaped wireless mouse that runs for more than a month on a single full charge. It is expected to be a groundbreaking input controller for everyday use of AR (augmented reality) glasses: lightweight, with little worry about running out of battery.  
(Press release)  
[https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/press/pr2025-10-01-003](https://www.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/press/pr2025-10-01-003)

**The problem**  
Small ring-shaped devices that can be used together with lightweight AR glasses can only carry a small battery because of physical constraints. Even with low-power wireless technologies such as BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy), continuous data transmission drained the battery in about 5 to 10 hours, requiring frequent charging.

**Key points and solution**  
In this research we developed an ultra-low-power wireless communication technology and achieved ultra-long operation of more than a month on a single full charge.

**1. Ultra-low power**  
We fundamentally redesigned the communication scheme, which accounted for most of the ring mouse's power consumption, cutting it to about 2% of conventional BLE.  
Between the ring and the wristband we adopted ultra-low-power magnetic backscatter communication, with a highly sensitive coil design that extends the communication range. Because information is sent simply by reflecting an external magnetic field, no power-hungry signal amplifier or oscillator is needed, and the device operates on very little power.

**2. Better communication performance at medium range**  
Whereas conventional backscatter such as NFC is limited to a range of 1–5 cm, this scheme achieves reliable communication at a medium range of about 12–14 cm. It combines a highly sensitive coil using distributed capacitors with a balanced bridge circuit that cancels noise.  
This extends the range of magnetic backscatter by about 2.1 times and gives robust communication performance against external electromagnetic noise.

**Outlook**  
This technology dramatically improves the usability of AR glasses. It is expected to trigger their transformation from a tool for particular settings into "a tool everyone uses every day", accelerating adoption. It also greatly advances the practical use of small wearable devices that power constraints had kept out of reach, contributing to the development of research on wearable wireless communication.
