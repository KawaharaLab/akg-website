On October 13, 2025, the "Intelligent Soft Wearables Workshop", organized by Project Assistant Professor Ryo Takahashi and colleagues, was held at ACM UbiComp/ISWC 2025. Under the theme of "AI-driven soft wearables", it was a valuable opportunity for researchers and students interested in the field to exchange ideas.

Held at Aalto University in Finland, the international workshop was planned by organizers from eight universities: the University of Tokyo, Cornell University, MIT Media Lab, University of Notre Dame, DFKI, Georgia Tech, University of Washington and National University of Singapore. This was its second edition.

Intelligent soft wearables are next-generation wearable devices that build intelligent functions such as sensors and electronic circuits into soft materials. Unlike conventional rigid devices, they are attracting attention as future devices that improve productivity while remaining comfortable.

The workshop consisted of talks and a demo and poster session. Project Assistant Professor Takahashi demonstrated "Plug-n-play e-knit with LED-based sensor positioning", a result that lets electronic textiles be handled like a breadboard. Participants tried Plug-n-play e-knit for themselves and exchanged views actively.

We will continue to advance research in this field and to create opportunities for broad exchange, aiming at the social implementation of soft wearables.
