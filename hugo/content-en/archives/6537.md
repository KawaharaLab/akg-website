The master's graduates with their diplomas

On March 24 and 25, 2026, the FY2025 diploma award and graduation ceremonies were held.  
From our laboratory, five master's students and four undergraduates, nine in all, received their degrees.  
The whole laboratory is delighted that the efforts of these members, who devoted themselves to their research throughout their time here, have borne fruit on this day.
