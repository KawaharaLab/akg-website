On May 22 and 23, 2026, we held a two-day research discussion retreat at the University of Tokyo's Yamanaka lodge.  
Our laboratory brings together researchers from many different fields. The retreat was held to engage deeply with one another's research, deepen exchange between groups, and further strengthen the laboratory's collective problem-solving ability.

The theme of the workshop was to grasp the trends and explore the open problems in each member's field. The fields shared ranged widely, from robotics and acoustic sensing to blockchain and fabrication. Each group shared the trends in its field and gave feedback from one another's perspectives.

![](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-3-1024x576.jpeg)

The first day started with listing concrete keywords within each field, based on the themes members had brought. Members with different specialties offered opinions from their own viewpoints while examining the trends and bottlenecks in each field. It was also a valuable opportunity to understand the research of members who usually work in different fields.

On the second day, members organized the problems they had narrowed down and, based on surveys of competing work in Japan and abroad, turned them into research plans and grant proposals. To wrap up, these were shared and discussed, with meaningful feedback on one another's plans.

![](/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/image-4-1024x768.jpeg)

The retreat also showed the laboratory's personality off duty. Ours is a lab with skilled musicians and many taco lovers: a piano performance during a break, and after the second day ended, a stop at a taco restaurant near Lake Yamanaka. It was the best possible ending, full of smiles, blowing away the fatigue of two days of intense discussion.
