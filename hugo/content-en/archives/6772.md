In FY2026 our laboratory welcomed a new Project Lecturer, Shintaro Shiba.  
Dr. Shiba is a researcher in computer vision and machine learning (AI), and an expert attracting worldwide attention for his research on event cameras, a next-generation visual sensor.  
He earned his doctorate in this field after training at the Technical University of Berlin (Germany) and Keio University. Building on the neuroscience he studied through his master's degree, he aims to realize new visual intelligence systems that are robust and energy-efficient, like living things.

He also brings extensive hands-on experience from the front lines of industry: as a Research Scientist and tech lead of the Vision AI, MLCore team at Woven by Toyota, Inc., he worked on research and development for automated driving and the Woven City project. Drawing on this experience as a "bridge between industry and academia" that connects research results to society, he is expected to bring new perspectives to supervision in our laboratory and to lead joint research.  
In his private life he plays jazz saxophone, a sign of a talented and active personality!

**Message**  
I am Shiba, and I joined this laboratory this academic year. The enthusiasm of the students has been a great stimulus for me. Drawing on my experience in corporate research and development, let us make an impact on society and lead the world through research that combines theory and practice.
