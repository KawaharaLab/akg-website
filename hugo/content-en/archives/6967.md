Project Lecturer Shintaro Shiba received the Sankei Shimbun Award at the 39th Advanced Technology Award of the Sankei Shimbun for the paper "Next-generation image processing and low-power AI enabled by asynchronous processing".  
The award ceremony was held on July 22, 2026 at the Meiji Kinenkan. (Awarded July 22, 2026)
