Low-Cost Soil Moisture Profile Probe  

Electrodes design for multiple depth soil moisture sensors.  

Copper electrodes fabricated on PET film.  

Low-cost soil moisture profile probe in farm field.

Precision farming considered as a pragmatic way to enhance farming productivity. In precision farming, collecting data should be crucial to optimize farming operation and various sensors are used. However, these sensors are mainly designed for research use so it usually have high accuracy but quite expensive. The cost would not be acceptable for farmers especially they intend to introduce such sensor system in large scale. Thus, we are developing a low-cost soil moisture profile probe composed by coplanar electrodes fabricated on thin PET film by printed electronics technologies and a cheap capacitive touch sensor widely used for touch panels. The probe can measure multiple soil moistures (10, 20, 30 cm) and temperatures (10, 20, 30 cm and air temperature), locally store sensed data and wirelessly send it to access point. We assume that the probe can be delivered with around 10 % cost of currently available sensors.

Yuki Kojima, Ryo Shigeta, Naoya Miyamoto, Yasutomo Shirahama, Kazuhiro Nishioka, Masaru Mizoguchi, Yoshihiro Kawahara, “Low-Cost Soil Moisture Profile Probe Using Thin-Film Capacitors and a Capacitive Touch Sensor”. Sensors 2016, vol.16, no.8, pp.1292, Aug. 2016.
