The Asami–Kawahara Laboratory will present research and technology exhibits at the University of Tokyo [May Festival](http://eeic.jp).

Alongside demonstrations of augmented reality and energy harvesting, visualization of radio waves and a Twitter diagnosis of your tweets, visitors can try their hand at building a terrestrial digital TV antenna and at [techno-shugei](http://techno-shugei.com/), electronic handicrafts.

For the techno-shugei session, [Yoshida](https://twitter.com/#!/tomo370) of the Techno-Shugei Club is expected to drop by.

・Open house schedule

- Friday, May 27 (department open day) 13:00–17:00
- Saturday, May 28 (May Festival) 11:00–17:00
- Sunday, May 29 (May Festival) 11:00–17:00

&nbsp;

- The core time for techno-shugei is Saturday the 28th, 15:00–17:00. It will run on the 29th if parts remain. If you would like to take part, please e-mail <mayfes@akg.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp>. The first ten or so groups will be given priority.

The antenna-building session and the other exhibits run throughout the open house.
