---
title: Engagement
titleJa: 社会連携
type: engagement
lede: We work with companies and other organisations to make technologies from materials to AI usable in the real world. Our activities range from small-budget joint research to organisational partnerships with active exchange of people, and starting companies based on research.
formsTitle: Ways to collaborate
forms:
- title: Joint research
  body: We undertake joint research starting with small-budget projects on specific technical questions. We work with companies on experiments, development and evaluation according to the purpose and scale of the research.
- title: Joint research chairs and divisions
  body: We consider joint research chairs and divisions well suited to sustained collaboration between organisations. They provide a setting where company researchers, university faculty, researchers and students interact regularly and work on shared questions.
- title: Starting companies from research
  body: We also start companies to bring laboratory technologies to people as products and services. We choose to develop a business according to the research and the needs of the people who will use it.
