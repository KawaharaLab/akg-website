imageCaption: "Grasping fragile bread without crushing it."
why:
  - >-
    Seeing and thinking alone do not change the world. Only when it moves, grasps and carries things has
    intelligence acted on the real world.
  - >-
    Yet rigid metal robots are hard to use near people or near fragile objects. We need mechanisms that act
    softly, lightly and safely.
approach:
  - >-
    Soft actuators driven by air or heat, transforming mechanisms built on origami structures, robots that move
    by exploiting the softness of their bodies, inflatable vehicles. We build the mechanisms of motion themselves
    from materials and structure.
  - >-
    We also develop ways to know the result of acting. Read what is being touched from the feel of it; judge
    whether the grasp succeeded from the sound inside the gripper. Mapping textures onto words like "rough" and
    "smooth" belongs here too.
