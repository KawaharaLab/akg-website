why:
  - >-
    An ordinary camera takes the same number of frames every second even when nothing is happening, then
    throws them away. Intelligence placed where power is scarce cannot afford that waste. What is needed is not
    to record everything but to catch only what has changed.
  - >-
    Helping human perception is the same problem. Pick one voice out of two that overlap. Tell where one word
    of sign language ends and the next begins. Each is a question of what to keep and what to discard.
approach:
  - >-
    At the centre is the event camera. Only pixels whose brightness changes emit a signal, so it uses little
    power and follows fast motion. We combine it with asynchronous processing and 3D reconstruction to recover
    shape with little computation.
  - >-
    In sound, we shift the speaking rate of voices to help listeners tell them apart, and build head-worn
    microphone arrays for people with single-sided deafness. In sign language recognition, we aim at recognising
    anyone's signing on a small computer.
